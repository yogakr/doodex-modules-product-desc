The gapWhat Odoo 17 does and does not give Purchase

Odoo already knows how to configure a product. It just never offers on a purchase order.

Everything the configurator needs is already in your database. Your products have attributes. Some of them have optional products attached. Odoo shows all of it on a quotation and none of it on a purchase order — so buyers retype what sales gets handed.

Ordering a configurable machine today

Standard Odoo 17 purchase order

Pick the exact variant from a dropdown — if the right one exists yet.
Nothing tells you the machine has three matching accessories.
Add each accessory as its own line, found by typing its name.
Attribute surcharges are invisible — nothing shows why one variant costs more.
No record on the line of the choices behind the variant.

How it worksFrom product template to priced order line

The mechanism

Four steps, one dialog

Everything happens on the purchase order line you are already filling in. Pick a product, answer the questions the product itself defines, and the order lines write themselves.

1
Pick the product template

Type the product name on the line as usual. Templates with variant attributes or optional products are the ones with something to configure.

2
Configure it

Choose the attribute values, type any free-text spec, and tick the accessories the product carries. Surcharges show beside the values that add cost.

3
See the supplier price

Prices come from the supplier lines on the product, converted into the order’s own currency.

4
Confirm, and the lines land

Every product you selected becomes its own order line, priced and described. A combination with no variant yet gets one created at that moment.

What it puts on the purchase order line

Eight things, all of them read from product data you already maintain. One dialog produces as many order lines as you tick.

The configurator dialog

Opens on the order line for any template that has variant attributes or optional products attached.

Optional products

The accessories on the template, listed with quantities, and attribute exclusion rules applied so an unbuildable combination is never offered.

Supplier-line pricing

Read from the supplier lines on the product, for the main product and every optional one alike.

The order's currency

Supplier prices are converted using the exchange rates already configured in your Odoo. No external rate service.

Attribute surcharges

Values that add cost display the surcharge next to them inside the dialog, so nobody has to work out why a total moved.

Free-text attribute values

Cut length, tolerance, finish — typed into the dialog and kept on the order line, so the supplier reads the words you agreed.

Dynamic variants

A combination that has no product variant yet gets one created when you confirm, instead of you pre-building records nobody has ordered.

A reopenable configuration

The attribute values, the free text and the no-variant extra prices stay on the line and come back exactly as you left them.

Every accessory offered, before you forget it

Optional products attached to the template are listed underneath, ready to tick into their own order lines. Exclusion rules hide combinations nobody can build, and dropping a parent drops the extras that only hung off it.

product.template → optional_product_ids
03

Priced from your supplier lines, not your sales catalogue

Every figure in the dialog — main product and accessories alike — is read from the supplier lines already on your products, then converted into the order’s currency. Several supplier lines resolve to the first one on the product; none at all falls back to the product’s cost.

product.supplierinfo → price, fallback standard_price
04

Buy in euros from a dollar price list

Supplier prices convert into the purchase order's own currency using the exchange rates already configured in your Odoo. The figure you approve in the dialog is the figure that lands on the order.

converted into purchase.order.currency_id
05

Variants born when someone actually orders them

Attributes set to create variants on demand stay uncreated until a buyer needs one. Confirm a never-ordered combination and the product variant appears on the line at that moment — no thousands of records built on spec.

create_variant = “dynamic”, created on confirm
06

Reopen a saved configuration, exactly as you left it

Attribute values, free-text specs and no-variant surcharges all stay on the purchase order line. Reopen the configurator and nothing is empty; swap the product and values that no longer apply clear themselves out.

SetupWhat it needs, what it does not

Requirements

Fifteen answers before you install

Odoo version

Odoo 17.0, Community or Enterprise. This listing is the 17.0 build; nothing here claims support for another version.

Other apps needed

Purchase, plus two standard Odoo modules: purchase_product_matrix and sale_product_configurator. Odoo installs both for you.

Installing

Drop the folder in your addons path, update the apps list, and it installs itself — it is marked auto-install.

Where it runs

Anywhere you are allowed to add a module: your own server, or Odoo.sh. Odoo Online does not accept custom modules on standard plans.

Setup after installing

None in the module. What matters is your product data: a template needs attributes or optional products before a configurator has anything to configure.

Your existing data

Untouched. It adds two fields to the purchase order line and one to the order. No existing purchase order is rewritten on install.

Vendor prices

Comes from the supplier lines already on your products. Fill those in and the configurator prices from them.

Currencies

Uses the exchange rates already configured in your Odoo. Nothing new to set up, no external rate service.

Product variants

Works with the variants you already have, and can create a dynamic variant at confirm time when the combination has none.

Your data

Nothing leaves your database. Every route the module adds is a local one in your own Odoo. No telemetry, no licence check.

Languages

42 translation files ship in the i18n folder, and the strings follow Odoo's own so they read naturally in whatever language your users have.

Tests

Included, not hidden: 5 Python test modules, 2 QUnit files and a browser tour. Run them on a staging database first.

Licence

LGPL-3. Free to use, free to read, free to adapt. The full licence text is in the download.

On phones

The configurator is part of the Odoo back office, so it follows the web client. We build and test it on desktop, where purchase orders are made.

If you ever remove it

Uninstall from Apps and purchase orders go back to standard behaviour. Because it is auto-install, take the folder out of your addons path so an apps-list update does not bring it back.

FAQThirteen questions buyers actually ask

Questions, answered without hedging

In one sentence, what does this module add?

It puts Odoo’s product configurator — attributes plus optional products — on purchase order lines, and prices what you configure from the supplier lines already on your products, in the order’s own currency.

Do I need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It depends on Purchase, purchase_product_matrix and sale_product_configurator, all of which come with standard Odoo. Community is enough.

Which Odoo versions does it support?

Odoo 17.0. That is what this build is and that is all we claim. A port to Odoo 18 is in development; when it is released it gets its own listing and its own version number, not a quiet edit to this page.

Is it really free, or free until version 2?

Free, under LGPL-3, with the source in the download. There is no paid edition of this module and no feature on this page is held back for one. We earn from Odoo services, and only if you ask for them.

Will it change my existing purchase orders?

No. Installing adds two fields to the purchase order line and one to the order, and adjusts which columns the line list shows by default. Orders you already have keep their products, prices and totals. There is no data migration to run.

Nothing happened after I installed it. Is it broken?

Almost certainly not. The configurator only opens when the product template you chose has variant attributes or optional products. A template with a single variant and no options behaves exactly as before, on purpose — nobody wants a dialog to click through for a box of screws. Add one attribute line or one optional product to a template and try that template on a new line.

Which price does the configurator show?

A price from the product’s supplier lines, converted into the order’s currency. Where a product carries more than one supplier line the configurator resolves to the first one on the product; where it carries none, it uses the product’s cost. The order is always that: first supplier line, then cost.

What happens if I change the vendor after configuring a line?

Lines already written keep the price they were created with, the same as any other purchase order line in Odoo. The configurator does not re-resolve a configured line against a different vendor, so where a vendor change should change your cost, set the line price yourself or rebuild the line.

Does it work with the variant grid we already use?

Yes, side by side. Templates you have set to matrix entry still open the grid. Everything else opens the configurator. The module chooses per product instead of forcing one behaviour on all of them.

Does anything leave my database?

No. The routes the module adds are local calls inside your own Odoo, serving the dialog with your own product data. There is no external service, no licence server, no analytics and no telemetry. You can confirm that yourself: it is LGPL-3 and the controller file is 342 lines.

Can we adapt it to our process?

Yes, and LGPL-3 gives you the right to. Your own developer can extend it the normal Odoo way. If you would rather we made the change, or need it ported to another Odoo version, ask and we will quote it.

How do I uninstall it, and does it come back?

Uninstall from Apps like any module; purchase orders return to standard behaviour. One thing to know: it is marked auto-install, which means Odoo installs it automatically whenever its dependencies are present — so an apps-list update can bring it back. To keep it off for good, remove the folder from your addons path as well.
