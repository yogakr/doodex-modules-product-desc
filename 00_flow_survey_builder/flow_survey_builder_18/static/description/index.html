What’s unique to Flow Survey Builder

Eight question types that do the work a text box cannot

Where Flow Survey Builder earns its $199: eight real Odoo question types, each with its own settings, live validation and readable answers in your database.

01 / 08Opinion Scale

Run NPS inside Odoo without a subscription. Choose the range, label the first, middle and last points, and the score lands on the response as a number you can filter, segment and report on like any other Odoo field.

02 / 08Rating

The friendliest question on the page, and still clean data underneath. Pick from 17 icon shapes — stars, hearts, smileys, thumbs, crowns — and set how many. Response rates go up; the answer is still a number you can average.

03 / 08Ranking

Stop guessing what matters most. Respondents drag your options into their own order and you get a ranked list per person — the fastest honest way to settle a roadmap, a feature vote or a shortlist.

04 / 08Matrix

Ask twelve things in the space of one. Rows are what you ask about, columns are the scale, and either grows with a single click. Every row is stored as its own readable answer, so the grid reports as cleanly as it reads.

05 / 08Meeting

Turn a completed survey into a booked call. A branded button to your own scheduling page, with the text, link and colour under your control — and the answer is recorded as Clicked or Not Clicked, so you can see exactly who is warm.

06 / 08File Upload

Collect CVs, photos, receipts and screenshots without a second tool. You set how many files, how large and which formats you accept; every file lands on the response inside Odoo where your team can open it.

07 / 08Multi Level Selection

Country, then region. Category, then product, then variant. A dependent hierarchy inside one question, so a long list stops being a wall of options and starts feeling like a decision.

08 / 08Database Selector

The question that only makes sense inside Odoo. Options are read live from your own records — products, customers, machines, projects — and the list can narrow itself based on an earlier answer. Nothing to copy, nothing going stale.

Fenced in by design

A public survey reaches only the business records you point it at — never your users, permission groups, company settings or mail templates. Record references from the browser are ignored, so the URL cannot be tampered with.

How the logic works

First match wins, and Back retraces the real path

Rules run in order, first match wins

Every slide has a default next slide plus your conditional rules. Conditions AND inside a rule, and the first match wins.

Ten operators, no formulas

Equals, not equals, contains, does not contain, starts with, ends with, and four ordering comparisons on numbers and dates.

Live in the browser, and reversible

Per question, rules are OR-ed and conditions AND-ed as the respondent answers — and Back retraces the path they actually took.

Checked twice, every time

Every answer is validated in the browser as the respondent types, then checked again on your server before it is saved — so nobody can slip past a required question or a character limit.

05/06FAQs

Fourteen questions buyers actually ask

Do I need the free Reader as well?

Yes, and you do not have to go and find it. Buy Flow Survey Builder and Odoo installs the free Flow Survey Reader alongside it automatically. Your total is $199, once.

Is $199 one-time or a subscription?

One time, per database. No per-response fee, no seat fee, nothing to renew. Unlimited surveys, questions and responses — you are bounded only by your own database and filestore.

What happens to my surveys if I uninstall the Builder?

They keep running, and answers keep arriving. The free Reader is what serves your surveys, and it holds all the data — the Builder only draws them. Take the Builder away and you lose the editor. Nothing else changes, and nothing is deleted.

Does it work on Odoo Community?

Yes. It only uses parts of Odoo that every Community install already has, so you do not need an Enterprise licence to run a single survey.

Which Odoo versions are supported?

Odoo 17, 18 and 19. This page is the Odoo 18 edition, and each version has its own build and its own user guide — not one manual stretched across three releases.

Can respondents answer without an Odoo account?

No account, no sign-up, nothing to install. Your online survey sits on a public link with a random 16-character code, so it cannot be guessed, and a survey you have not published yet simply is not there.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Your surveys, your answers and every uploaded file stay in your own Odoo. We never send them anywhere, and neither does the module. A few styling files — icons, country flags, the text-editor toolbar — load from the same public content networks thousands of ordinary websites use, so those networks see that a browser asked for an icon, never a survey answer. If your server is offline or locked down and you want those files served from your own Odoo instead, ask us and we will set that up.

Can I move a survey to another database?

Yes. Export the survey to a single file from the Builder, then import it on the other database. The design, every slide and question, the logic, the branching and even the images all travel inside that one file — nothing to re-upload. Answers stay behind, so you are copying the survey, not the data.

Can a survey create a CRM lead automatically?

Yes. On the Workflow tab, chain Create Record into Create Activity and Send Email, and map answers into the lead's fields. No code. You can branch the action chain with a Decision node.

Can I test a survey before publishing it?

Yes. The Test button opens the live survey in test mode, even while the survey is in Draft, and walks the complete flow including submission. Test submissions are stored but flagged as Test Entry, so you can filter or delete them.

Is there a results dashboard?

No. Results are participant and answer lists, using Odoo's standard grouping, filtering and spreadsheet export. If you need charts, build them with Odoo's own reporting on those records.

Can I control who inside Odoo sees the results?

Yes. There are two groups: User Flow Survey and Administrator Flow Survey. Users can read all survey definitions but only edit and see the results of surveys they created. Administrators see everything. Anyone in neither group has no access to survey data.

Can I extend it?

Yes. Your developer can extend any part of it the normal Odoo way — the data, the public pages, the editor itself — without forking the module. A full developer guide comes with it, and if you would rather we built the change, ask us.

Can I make the survey match our brand?

That is most of what you are buying. You set the logo and where it sits, pick from four font families, control text sizes and alignment, background images and colours, the panel styling, and the button colours and corner shapes — separately for the cover page, the question slides and the thank-you page. Selected and unselected answers get their own colours too. And if you configure nothing at all, the survey quietly picks up your own Odoo's accent colour, so it looks like yours from the first install.
